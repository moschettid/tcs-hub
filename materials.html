Learning Materials

Curated resources for studying theoretical computer science

Acknowledgment

This page compiles resources from the Awesome Theoretical Computer Science repository, curated and maintained by Mostafa Touny and the community.

📚  Introductory Resources

  • Introduction to Theoretical Computer Science

    Author: Boaz Barak

    A free online textbook covering the mathematical foundations of computer science.

    View Resource →

  • Theory of Computation

    Author: Michael Sipser

    Classic textbook on automata theory, computability, and complexity theory.

🔬  Computational Complexity

  • Computational Complexity: A Modern Approach

    Authors: Sanjeev Arora & Boaz Barak

    Comprehensive graduate-level textbook on complexity theory.

    View Resource →

  • The Nature of Computation

    Authors: Cristopher Moore & Stephan Mertens

    An accessible introduction to computational complexity and the P vs NP problem.

🧮  Algorithms

  • Introduction to Algorithms (CLRS)

    Authors: Cormen, Leiserson, Rivest, Stein

    The definitive textbook on algorithms and data structures.

  • Algorithm Design

    Authors: Jon Kleinberg & Éva Tardos

    Covers algorithm design techniques with real-world applications.

  • Algorithms

    Authors: Dasgupta, Papadimitriou, Vazirani

    Clear and accessible introduction to algorithms with emphasis on understanding.

    View Resource →
📊  Randomized Algorithms

  • Randomized Algorithms

    Authors: Rajeev Motwani & Prabhakar Raghavan

    Comprehensive coverage of probabilistic algorithms and their analysis.

  • Probability and Computing

    Authors: Michael Mitzenmacher & Eli Upfal

    Randomized algorithms with focus on probabilistic techniques.

🎯  Approximation Algorithms

  • The Design of Approximation Algorithms

    Authors: David Williamson & David Shmoys

    Modern treatment of approximation algorithms for NP-hard problems.

    View Resource →
🔐  Cryptography

  • Introduction to Modern Cryptography

    Authors: Jonathan Katz & Yehuda Lindell

    Rigorous treatment of cryptography with proofs of security.

  • A Graduate Course in Applied Cryptography

    Authors: Dan Boneh & Victor Shoup

    Free online textbook covering modern cryptographic techniques.

    View Resource →
🎲  Quantum Computing

  • Quantum Computation and Quantum Information

    Authors: Michael Nielsen & Isaac Chuang

    The standard textbook for quantum computing and information theory.

  • Quantum Computing Since Democritus

    Author: Scott Aaronson

    Accessible introduction to quantum computing with philosophical perspectives.

🎮  Game Theory & Mechanism Design

  • Algorithmic Game Theory

    Editors: Nisan, Roughgarden, Tardos, Vazirani

    Comprehensive coverage of computational aspects of game theory.

    View Resource →
